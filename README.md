### Personal Projects
My stance is that, with more and more powerful 'AI' models, we are entering an age of what I would call personalized software. If you are not happy with a preexisting software solution for your needs, you can simply create one with the help of 'AI'. That being said, I of course would not condone use of these solutions for commercial applications, but for light personal use, they are just fine - both functional and secure enough. I believe that as this will accelerate, it will be possible to get by with one cheap subscription for project planning and management (with a frontier model) and easy-to-run local models for actual coding and implementation.
